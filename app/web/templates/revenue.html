{# ==================================================================== 3. REVENUE CONTENT TYPES (Blueprint Section 7.3 reference) ==================================================================== #}

Revenue Content Types

Five categories of revenue-driving social content from the playbook. Use these as templates when creating or reviewing posts.

$ Cost-of-Guessing

Quantifies the financial cost of not using NTangible data. Makes the status quo feel expensive.

Example "Failed D1 transfer: -$150K. Draft bust: -$3.5M. What if you had pressure-performance data before the decision?"
✓ Social Proof Sequences

Showcases why partners and clients chose NTangible. Builds credibility through real decisions.

Example "Here's why [Partner] chose NTangible over guessing. Their scouting staff now has pressure-performance context on every prospect."
¶ Case Study Drops

Short-form verified client stories. Real outcomes, specific numbers, no fluff.

Example "[University] used Clutch Factor data to evaluate 47 transfer portal entries. Three red flags identified. Two offers pulled. Zero regrets."
→ Assessment CTA Posts

Direct call-to-action driving assessment sign-ups. Clear value proposition, simple next step.

Example "Find out your Clutch Factor. 20 minutes. One assessment. A data profile that travels with you. Link in bio."
≠ Competitive Positioning

Differentiates NTangible without naming competitors. Frames the category so the audience self-selects.

Example "Most mental performance tools give you a score. We give you a profile that predicts behavior under pressure. There's a difference."
